# Traffic sign images

Spanish traffic signs sourced from Wikimedia Commons. Spanish traffic
signs are works of the Spanish government (DGT) and, per Ley 22/1987
de Propiedad Intelectual (art. 13), are not protected by copyright.

Each SVG here is downloaded from Wikimedia Commons via:
`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Spain_traffic_signal_<code>.svg`

Files:

- `p4.svg`   — P-4   Roundabout ahead.
- `p13a.svg` — P-13a Dangerous curve right.
- `p13b.svg` — P-13b Dangerous curve left.
- `p20.svg`  — P-20  Pedestrian crossing ahead.
- `p24.svg`  — P-24  Children.
- `r1.svg`   — R-1   Ceda el paso (yield).
- `r2.svg`   — R-2   STOP.
- `r101.svg` — R-101 No entry.
- `r301.svg` — R-301 Speed limit.
- `r400a.svg` — R-400a Mandatory direction (straight).
- `r402.svg` — R-402 Roundabout direction.
- `s13.svg`  — S-13  Pedestrian crossing (info).
